About This Portal

This educational website provides organized learning materials to help students understand obligations, contracts, legal responsibilities, and the consequences of non-performance.

Learning Modules

Lesson 1: Obligations

Understand the meaning, essential elements, and basic concepts of obligations.

Lesson 2: Sources of Obligations

Learn the five sources of obligations under Article 1157 of the Civil Code.

Lesson 3: Contracts

Study consent, object, and cause, the essential requisites under Article 1318.

Lesson 4: Breach of Obligations

Explore the legal consequences of failing to fulfill an obligation.

Practical Examples

Borrowing Money

Ana borrows ₱1,000 from Ben and agrees to repay it. Their agreement may create an obligation to repay the amount.

Buying a Product

A buyer agrees to pay for a product, while the seller agrees to deliver it.
